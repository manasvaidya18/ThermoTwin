ThermoTwin: status and plan

Team Alebaple · CODE APEX 3.0, Track 4 (UST) · T4-1 Healthcare cold chain · Status as of 21 October 2026 · Grand finale 24 October 2026
In one paragraph. ThermoTwin is a software early-warning layer for vaccine fridges. It reads the temperature that existing loggers (eVIN) already record, estimates the vaccine's own temperature with a per-fridge digital twin (the Virtual Vial), predicts freezing 1–2 hours ahead with a LightGBM risk model, and tells staff the likely cause and what to do. WHO alarms keep running unchanged. Research, PRD, pitch deck, a clickable demo UI and a working core system are done. On simulated data, ThermoTwin warns a median of 90 minutes before the vaccine reaches 0 °C, where the WHO rule fires 30 minutes after, with 0.48 false alerts per fridge-month. The next three days connect the model to the UI, add the heat and cause models, and validate.

1. The problem

Vaccines must stay at +2 to +8 °C. Freeze-sensitive vaccines (pentavalent, hepatitis B, DTP-containing, PCV, IPV) are permanently damaged at or below about −0.5 °C, and a frozen vial looks normal. Indian studies found vaccines below 0 °C for roughly 10–15% of storage time. Today's WHO alarms fire only after damage starts (10 h above +8 °C, or 1 h at or below −0.5 °C). India's ~25,000 eVIN loggers across about 29,000 cold-chain points (UNDP) already record every fridge, but the data is used for stock, not prediction.

Ways cooling goes out of control: too cold (thermostat set or drifting cold, relay stuck on, ice-lined cold spots, overshoot after a power cut); too warm (power cut, door left open, warm stock, heat waves); slow decline (compressor wear, refrigerant leak, worn gasket, dusty condenser, ice build-up); and a lying sensor (stuck, drifting, dropouts).

3. Architecture

Data in (existing loggers, eVIN) → ingest and trust gate → digital twin with fleet priors (Virtual Vial) → LightGBM risk engine → rule-based alert tiers (Sensor check, Normal, Advisory, Early warning, Critical) → staff app, SMS/voice, technician ticket, bounded copilot → technician feedback and recalibration. Observed alarms are never gated by a forecast; the LLM only rewords Cause Cards.

How the core pieces work

PieceMethod
Simulator (ColdChain Gym)3-node RC heat model (air, product, enclosure) at 1-minute steps, thermostat with hysteresis, clinic-hour door openings, Indian room temperatures, and 7 injected faults (thermostat drifting cold, relay stuck on, compressor wear, gasket leak, power cut, stuck probe, drifting probe). Logs every 10 minutes like eVIN.
Digital twin / Virtual VialKalman filter per fridge, state [air, slope, vial]. The vial follows Newton's law of cooling: dTvial/dt = (Tair − Tvial)/τ, with τ = mc/hA from the fridge-class prior. Outputs the vial estimate with an error band and 30/60/120-minute physics forecasts.
Risk modelLightGBM discrete-time hazard over twelve 10-minute slots; inputs are temperature-only features plus twin outputs. Gives calibrated 1-h and 2-h risk (isotonic), time-to-breach P10/P50/P90, and SHAP drivers for the Cause Card.
LabelsFreeze event = true vaccine temperature ≤ −0.5 °C for 60 min (from simulator ground truth only). Lead time is measured to the true vaccine crossing 0 °C.
Alert thresholdChosen on validation fridges as the most sensitive setting within a budget of 1 false alert per fridge-month.

4. Results so far (simulated, held-out fridges)

240 fridges × 21 days (5,040 fridge-days), 178 freeze events. Split by fridge: 60% train, 20% validation, 20% test (48 fridges, 41 events).

Reading the result. ThermoTwin warns about two hours earlier than the WHO rule, within the false-alert budget. The simple early rule warns sooner but would send about five false SMS per fridge per month, which is the alarm fatigue we are designed to avoid. The Virtual Vial tracks the true vaccine temperature with 0.08 °C mean error (raw air: 0.46 °C), and its 2-sigma band covers 91% of cases (target 95%).

Honesty notes. These are simulator results, not field results. The simulator is not yet checked against public fridge traces or a second simulator. One realism change was made: a quarter of fridges run close to 2 °C, as real clinics often do; this makes the simple rules look worse and is disclosed in the code. The deck's illustrative chart (61 min before vs 77 min after) should be replaced by these measured numbers.

RiskMitigation

Organiser data in an unknown format or temperature-onlyIngest accepts DS01.2 JSON and CSV; the model already uses temperature only.
Demo fails on stageOffline UI with no internet dependency; recorded replay as fallback; scripted short path.
Over-claimingSay "designed to warn earlier" until validated; never "WHO-certified", "first AI cold-chain system" or "decides vaccine safety".
TimeCore path is frozen and working; new features only after the API and UI wiring land.
