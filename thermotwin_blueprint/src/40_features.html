DataColdChain Gym: the simulator that trains and tests everything

Track 4 is designed for simulated data with a hidden test set of unseen fault combinations. So we build a realistic generator, train on part of it, and test on conditions the model never saw, including a second, independent generator.

DimensionWhat we vary
EquipmentPQS compression fridge, ice-lined refrigerator (ice node, cold zone near the base), domestic fridge, deep freezer, walk-in cold room, solar direct-drive with battery
EnvironmentIndian seasonal ambient (cool winters to 45 °C heat waves), day and night cycles, room heat load
PowerOutage frequency and duration by setting (urban, rural), voltage fluctuation effects, solar battery decline
UseImmunisation session days, morning withdrawals, restocking with warm stock, door left ajar
FaultsThermostat drift to freezing, refrigerant leak, compressor wear, gasket leak, condenser fouling, evaporator icing or missed defrost, sudden failure, overload
SensorsSpikes, stuck values, drift, dropouts, missing data, noise levels
LabelsBreach and freeze onset, WHO FRZE and HEAT events, cause labels, non-functional windows, Virtual Vial ground truth

Splits that mimic the hidden test

  • Split by unit, never by random rows.
  • Leave-one-fault-family-out.
  • Unseen fault combinations, doubled noise, 20% missing data.
  • External test on a second generator: the synthetic fridge dataset and generator of Benamirouche et al. (2026), if its code is usable.

Real-data sanity checks (licences to confirm)

  • Bangkok in-fridge temperature data, 123 households at 5-minute intervals.
  • REFIT appliance power data for realistic compressor cycling.
  • ORNL display-case fault data (door-open and icing faults).

Scoring rules: merge consecutive alerts into episodes before counting; event-level and range-based precision and recall; no point-adjusted F1, which inflates scores. Ablations: without the twin, without the Virtual Vial, without two-key, without the trust gate, without calibration. All targets are goals until measured.

Positioning

This node is not WHO-prequalified. Pitch it as a predictive add-on beside the certified PQS or eVIN logger, never a replacement. The certified logger remains the official record, and humans decide what happens to any stock.
